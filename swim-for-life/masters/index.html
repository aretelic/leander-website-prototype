Swim for Life

Masters

Coached swimming for adults, whatever your reason for being in the water

Masters is Leander's squad for adults aged 18 and over. Get fitter, train for a triathlon, or get back in the pool after a few years away, with qualified coaches on poolside.

Try your first Masters session

Where Masters sits on the Leander pathway

BeforeAny route in: Diamond or a competitive squad, another club, triathlon, or years away from the pool
This squadMasters: adults 18+
Where it can leadYour own goals: fitness, triathlon, open water do Masters swimmers enter Masters galas? · Volunteering and becoming an official (Volunteer & become an official)

Who it's for

  • Adults aged 18 and over.
  • Fitness swimmers who want structured, coached sessions instead of swimming lengths on their own.
  • Triathletes who want to improve their swim, with coaches who specialise in triathlon.
  • Returners: former club swimmers, or anyone getting back in the water after time away.
  • Ability: minimum swimming ability for Masters, e.g. can swim a set distance of front crawl

What it's like

Two coached sessions a week. A coach plans each session and gives you feedback on your stroke, so every length has a purpose.

Coming back after a break? You don't need to be fast. Tell the coach about your swimming background and they'll help you find the right lane.

For triathletes

Our coaches include triathlon specialists, so they understand what you need from the swim leg.

Questions people ask

I haven't swum for years. Is Masters for me?

Yes. Returners are very welcome. Tell us your background when you get in touch and we'll help you find the right lane.

Do I have to race?

No. Many Masters swimmers train for fitness. is there an option to race at Masters galas for those who want to?

I'm training for a triathlon. Will the sessions help?

Yes. Our coaches include triathlon specialists.
