Swim for Life

Inclusive by design

Swimmers with a disability train in our squads, alongside everyone else

At Leander, swimmers with a disability, including swimmers with Down Syndrome, are part of our squads. Same pool, same coaches, same team. We talk to every family about what each swimmer needs, and we build from there.

What "inclusive by design" means

We don't run a separate programme. Swimmers with a disability join the squad or Academy stage that suits their swimming, and train with everyone else. That's a deliberate choice, and it's how Amalia trains.

What that looks like

  • A conversation first. Before a swimmer joins, we talk with the family about their swimming, their goals and anything that helps them train well.
  • The right group. We find the squad or Academy stage that fits the swimmer's ability and goals, using the same pathway as every swimmer. See The Leander pathway.
  • Coaches who know disability swimming. Several of our coaches hold qualifications in coaching swimmers with a disability (see below).
  • Support from welfare. Our Welfare Officers are there for every swimmer and family. We follow Wavepower, Swim England's safeguarding policy.
  • Racing, if they want it. how swimmers with a disability enter galas and classified competitions, and who at the club helps with this

The people

Edgar Andrade, Head Coach
Edgar has coached since 2001. As a performance coach he coached an Olympian and a Paralympian, and he has also coached disability swimming.

Caroline Findlay, Coach
Caroline has coached at Leander for 25 years. She is qualified to work with disabled performers and with children with special educational needs (SEN), and she helps at disability swimming events.

Gregory Kat, Coach
Greg has coached at Leander for 6 years and is qualified to teach disability swimming. He also holds a BSc in Sports Science.

Ruth Lock and Linda Kershaw, Welfare Officers
Ruth and Linda look after the welfare of every swimmer at the club. Contact them at welfare@leanderswim.com.

How to start a conversation

Every swimmer is different, so we start by talking.

  1. Get in touch. Use Join us and choose "swimmers with a disability", or email admin@leanderswim.com. named contact for inclusion enquiries, if different from admin
  2. Tell us about the swimmer. Their age, how they swim now, what they'd like from swimming, and anything that helps them in a new setting. Share only what you're comfortable with.
  3. We'll talk it through. A coach will talk to you about where your swimmer might fit. confirm next step, e.g. a call with a coach, then a trial session or assessment
  4. Try it. what a first session or trial looks like

We'll be honest with you about space: places in the Academy and some squads are limited, and there may be a waiting list.

Not the right fit yet? We'll point you in the right direction

Sometimes another organisation is a better starting point, for example a learn-to-swim group for children with additional needs, or a club with a dedicated para-swimming section. If that's the case, we're happy to signpost you to partners we know.
