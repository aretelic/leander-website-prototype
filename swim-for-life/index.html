Keep swimming, your way, and stay part of the team

Not every swimmer wants to race every weekend, and that's fine. Swim for Life is for teenagers who want to keep fit with their friends, swimmers easing off for exams, adults coming back to the water, and swimmers with a disability who want a club that takes them seriously.

Try your first Masters sessionAsk about Diamond

Three ways to swim for life

Diamond

For teenagers who want to keep swimming without the competition load. That includes squad swimmers who need to reduce training for GCSEs or A levels and come back afterwards.

About Diamond

Masters

For adults aged 18 and over. Fitness swimmers, triathletes and anyone returning to the water, coached twice a week.

About Masters

Inclusive by design

Swimmers with a disability, including swimmers with Down Syndrome, train within our squads. Every swimmer has a pathway here.

How inclusion works at Leander
