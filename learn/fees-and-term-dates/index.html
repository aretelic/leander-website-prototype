What does it cost, and what do you get?

Join the Academy waiting listBook a squad trial

The short answer

Every member pays a £48.75 annual membership. On top of that, Academy families pay a termly fee and squad families pay a squad fee for their swimmer's squad. Leander is not-for-profit and volunteer-run, so every fee goes back into the club.

Refunds and leaving: a summary

  • Changed your mind? You have 14 days after joining to cancel. Tell us in writing or by email. If your swimmer has swum during those 14 days, we'll charge for that part of the term or month and refund the rest of any fees you've paid.
  • Academy: after the 14 days, term fees aren't refundable.
  • Squads: after the 14 days, we need one month's notice if your swimmer is leaving. Fees are still due during the notice month.
  • Joining fees for the Academy and squads aren't refundable.

Read the full refund and cancellation policy

What this means for you

  • Budget for the £48.75 membership plus your Academy or squad fee, and for gala entries once your swimmer starts racing.
  • Check the minimum and recommended sessions for your squad, so you know the weekly commitment as well as the cost.
  • If you're unsure, email admin@leanderswim.com before you commit. You also have 14 days to change your mind.
