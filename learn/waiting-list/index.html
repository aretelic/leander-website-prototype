Learn to Swim

Join the Academy waiting list

Add your child's details and we'll be in touch when a place may be coming up. It takes about three minutes.

Go to the formAbout the Academy

What happens next

  1. You join the list using the form below. We'll email to confirm we've got it.
  2. When a place may be coming up, we'll contact you to arrange a short trial, so we can see which stage suits your child. Trials run throughout the year. confirm how families are contacted and how the trial works
  3. If there's a place, your child starts in the right stage, and you pay the £48.75 annual membership plus the termly fee. See Fees and term dates.

An honest note on waiting times. Leander is popular and places are limited. A typical wait is typical wait for an Academy place. We'd rather tell you now than surprise you later.
