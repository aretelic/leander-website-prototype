Squads

Book a squad trial

If your swimmer can already swim well and wants to train with a team, a trial is the place to start. Here's how it works, from the form to the coach's recommendation.

Request a trialSquad standards

Who should trial

A squad trial is for swimmers who can already swim confidently and want to train in a competitive group. That might be:

  • a swimmer from another club who's moving to the area or looking for a new club
  • a swimmer from a school squad
  • a confident swimmer who's never raced before but wants to start
  • a teenager looking to swim without the competition load in Diamond confirm Diamond is open to new joiners via trial

Not the right page?

  • Ages 5–10 still learning to swim: join the Academy waiting list instead.
  • Already in our Academy: Pre-Squad swimmers are assessed for a squad place by our coaches, so there's no need to book.
  • Adults 18+: see Masters.
  • Swimmers with a disability: you're very welcome to trial. Tell us about any support your swimmer needs on the form, or see Inclusive by design.

A rough guide: to join our first competitive squads, a swimmer should be able to swim 200m freestyle without stopping. See what each squad expects on Squad standards. confirm the minimum standard and age range for a squad trial, including Sparks

What happens at a trial

  1. Request a trial using the form below.
  2. We get in touch to arrange a date and venue. how soon families hear back, and who contacts them
  3. The trial. Your swimmer joins a coach in the pool. trial length, venue, whether it's a one-to-one assessment or part of a squad session
  4. What the coach looks at: the four strokes (freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke and butterfly), how far your swimmer can swim without stopping, dives and turns, and how they respond to coaching. Nobody expects perfection. Head Coach to confirm what is assessed
  5. A quick chat. You can ask questions about squads, sessions, fees and galas. confirm parents can talk to the coach on the day

Is there a cost? whether trials are free

What to bring

  • Swimming costume
  • Goggles
  • Swimming hat
  • Towel and a water bottle
  • Any recent PBs (personal best times) and the meets they were swum at, if your swimmer has raced
  • anything else, e.g. arriving 10 minutes early, footwear for poolside

Not got a Leander hat? No problem. Club kit comes later. See Kit by stage.

What happens after

  • We'll tell you the outcome and, if there's a place, which squad we recommend and why. how and when the outcome is shared
  • If there's a place: you'll get details of sessions, fees and how to join. Fees: see Fees and what they buy.
  • If there isn't a place right now: we'll explain whether your swimmer can go on a waiting list for the right squad. how the squad waiting list works
  • If another club would suit your swimmer better (for example, for travel or the level they're aiming at), we'll say so honestly and suggest options.

An honest note about places

Squad places can be limited, and we'd rather tell you that up front than over-promise. Places depend on the squad, the sessions and the time of year, and we can't always offer one straight away. current squad capacity and how families are prioritised
