Squads

Club records

What's the fastest a Leander swimmer has ever swum? Here are our records, and how they work.

Book a squad trialRace the Standards

The short answer

A club record is the fastest time a Leander swimmer has swum in an event, at a recognised competition, while swimming for Leander. We keep separate records for each event, age group, gender and pool length, plus open records for the fastest time at any age.

Leander swimmers set 29 club records across the main championships in 2025/26. Some of our history goes back to the 1950s.

How records work

Short course and long course

Records are kept separately for short course (SC: a 25m pool) and long course (LC: a 50m pool), because times in a 25m pool are usually faster (more turns and push-offs).

Age groups

Each record is set within an age group, so younger swimmers have records to aim for too. list Leander's record age groups (e.g. 10 and under, 11, 12 … 17, open) and the date used to work out age

Open records

An open record is the fastest time by any Leander swimmer in that event, whatever their age. confirm Leander keeps open records and how they are defined

What counts

Naming

To protect young swimmers, records will show first name and surname initial only. This prototype shows no names at all.

What this means for your swimmer

Club records are something to aim for at every age, not just for our fastest seniors. If your swimmer thinks they've broken a record, tell their coach. how a possible new record is reported and confirmed
