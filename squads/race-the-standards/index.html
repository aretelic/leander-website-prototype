Race the Standards

How close is your swimmer to a county time? Pop in a PB (personal best) and watch it race the standards.

Race your PBBook a squad trial

The short answer

Choose an age, gender, pool length, stroke and distance, then enter your swimmer's PB. The tool races it against the Surrey county qualifying and consideration times for the Surrey County Age Group Championships 2027, so you can see which times your swimmer is already inside and how much time there is still to find.

It also races the PB against the official London regional and national qualifying times, so you can see the next steps too.

The tool

Race the StandardsOpen the tool in a new tab ↗

How to enter a time: minutes:seconds.hundredths, for example 38.50 or 1:12.04.

Privacy: the tool runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to the club. It remembers your last choices on this device only.

Explained

Qualifying times and consideration times

To enter a championship, a swimmer needs to have swum fast enough at an earlier competition.

TimeWhat it means
Qualifying timeThe time you need to swim to enter that championship. Swim it (or faster) and you can enter
Consideration timeA slightly slower time that may still earn a place if there's space once all the qualifiers are in. It's not guaranteed

A swim only counts if it was at a licensed meet: a competition licensed by Swim England, from Level 4 (for newer racers) up to Level 1 (national level). confirm the qualifying window (dates) and licence levels accepted for Surrey County Championships 2027

Short course (SC) and long course (LC)

ItemShort course (SC)Long course (LC)
Pool length25 metres50 metres
TurnsMore turns per raceHalf as many turns
TimesUsually faster, because turns and push-offs add speedUsually slower for the same swimmer

Most UK pools are 25m, so many swimmers, especially younger ones, have more SC times than LC times. Always compare like with like: choose the pool length the PB was swum in. confirm whether Surrey accepts both SC and LC times for 2027 qualifying, and whether conversions are used

Age groups

County times are set by age group: 10/11, then each year from 12 to 17, then 18 and over. There are no county times for swimmers under 10. Surrey uses a swimmer's age at 31 December 2027.

Gender

The tool uses the county categories: Female and Male / Open.

Events

The tool covers 17 individual events: 50m to 1500m freestyle; 50m, 100m and 200m backstroke, breaststroke and butterfly; and 200m and 400m individual medley (IM: all four strokes in one race).

Where the regional and national times come from

  • County: the Surrey County Age Group Championships 2027 qualifying and consideration times. Age is worked out as at 31 December 2027.
  • Regional: the Swim England London Region Summer Championships 2026 qualifying times, which have separate tables for 25m and 50m pools.
  • National: the National Winter Championships 2026 qualifying times (25m pool), for swimmers aged 11 and over.

The National Summer Meet is by invitation, based on rankings, so it has no qualifying times. update to the 2027 regional and national times when they're published

Reading the results

  • A tick and a plus number (✓ +1.20): your swimmer is that many seconds inside the time.
  • A minus number (−0.85): that's the time still to drop.
  • "Next target": the next standard up, and how far away it is.

What to do next

Already inside a county time?

  • Talk to your swimmer's coach about which events to enter. how county entries are made (TeamUnify or coach-led) and the 2027 entry deadline
  • See what race day is like on How galas work.

Close to a time?

  • Ask the coach which upcoming licensed galas are good chances to have a go. See calendar.
  • Remember: a bad race is an event, not an identity. Most swimmers get there with consistent training, not one lucky swim.

Not near a county time yet?

  • That's completely normal, especially for younger swimmers. In our younger squads the focus is on enjoyment, PBs and better technique, not rankings.
  • See what each squad works on in Squad standards.

Not at Leander yet?

  • Book a squad trial and bring your swimmer's PBs with you.

What this means for your swimmer

County times are one useful marker, not the only one. Platinum asks for a county qualifying time and Mercury asks for regional qualifying times, but every squad also looks at technique, training and attitude.
