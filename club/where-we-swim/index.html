Where we swim

Leander isn't tied to one pool. We train across a network of pools in South-West and South London, which means more pool time, more session choices and room for a club of more than 500 swimmers to grow.

Join usSession times

Why a network of pools helps your swimmer

  • More water time. Using several pools lets us run more sessions each week, for more swimmers.
  • The right pool for the job. Different pools suit different sessions, from Academy lessons to squad training.
  • Closer to home. With pools from Tooting to Dulwich, there's often a session not far from you.
