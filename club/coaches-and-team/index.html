Coaches and team

These are the people who plan the sessions, run the galas and look after your swimmer.

Our coaches and teachers are qualified professionals. Our committee are volunteers. Together, they make Leander work. How the club works

Join usHow the club works

Our coaches

Edgar Andrade

Head Coach

Edgar has been Leander's Head Coach since 2022. A former Portuguese youth international (1998–99), he has coached since 2001 and holds a Level 3 coaching qualification from Portugal, recognised across the EU. As a performance coach from 2014 to 2016, he coached an Olympian and a Paralympian. He co-founded a swimming club in Portugal, and his experience reaches well beyond performance squads: he has also coached disability swimming, Masters swimmers and triathletes.

Email: Edgar's club email address (the current site lists a personal address)

Filipe Alves

Head of Academy Swimming and Coach

Filipe has led the Leander Academy, our learn-to-swim programme for ages 5–10, since September 2025. He was Portuguese national champion from 1999, represented Portugal internationally and won more than 50 national medals. He holds a degree in Sports Science, specialising in swimming, and has coached since 2008, including athletes who went on to win Olympic medals. He now brings that experience to children taking their first strokes.

Email: Filipe's club email address (the current site lists Caroline Findlay's)

Caroline Findlay

Coach

Caroline has coached at Leander for 25 years. For more than 20 years she has also been Lambeth's team manager at the London Youth Games, a multi-sport competition between London's boroughs. She holds a diploma in sports psychology and is qualified to work with disabled performers and with children with special educational needs (SEN). She also assists with disability swimming events, helping to make inclusion part of everyday life at the club.

Email: caroline.findlay@leanderswim.com

Gregory Kat

Coach

Gregory has coached at Leander for 6 years. He holds a BSc in Sports Science and is qualified as an Aquatics and Exercise coach and as a Level 3 personal trainer, so he understands fitness both in and out of the water. He is also qualified to teach disability swimming, which supports our approach of welcoming swimmers with a disability into our mainstream squads.

Email: Gregory's club email address

Anthony Owen

Coach

Anthony is qualified as a Level 2 teacher and a Level 2 coach. That means he can teach swimmers who are still learning the strokes, and coach swimmers who are training to race, so he understands both ends of the Leander pathway. Away from poolside, Anthony is an open-water and cold-water swimmer, which means swimming in lakes, rivers and the sea, often through the colder months of the year.

Email: Anthony's club email address

Strength and conditioning partner: Move4Sport

S&C (strength and conditioning) is training on land to support swimming: building strength, mobility and resilience, and reducing the risk of injury.

Our S&C partner is Move4Sport, based in West Wickham. Their Head Coach, Gareth Shelbourne, is UKSCA (UK Strength and Conditioning Association) S&C Coach of the Year 2026.
