Swimming glossary

New to swimming? You'll hear a lot of new words on poolside. Here's what they all mean, in plain English.

Join usThe pathway

A

Academy
Our learn-to-swim programme for ages 5–10. It has six stages plus Pre-Squad, and is taught at Ernest Bevin College in Tooting, South London.

Academy gala
A friendly competition for Academy swimmers, often their first taste of racing. Older squad swimmers can gain officiating experience at these galas.

Arena League
A team competition between swimming clubs, where swimmers score points for their club rather than only for themselves.

Assessment
How Academy teachers check a swimmer is ready to move up to the next stage.

B

Block start
A racing dive from the starting block at the end of the lane. Taught in Academy Stage 6.

C

Club record
The fastest time ever swum by a Leander swimmer in a given event, age group and pool length. Some of ours date back to the 1950s.

Consideration time
A slightly slower time than the qualifying time. It may still earn a place at a championship if there's space.

County championships (Counties)
The first level of championship racing. Ours are the Surrey County Championships.

County / regional / national qualifying time
The time a swimmer needs to swim to enter that championship. Also called a QT.

D

Development camps
Training camps run as part of the Swim England Talent pathway: County Development, Regional Development and National Age Group development camps. Swimmers are selected on their results and World Aquatics points. You can't apply.

Diamond
Our non-competitive squad for swimmers who want to keep training without the racing. Great for teenagers easing off around GCSEs and A levels. Swimmers can move back to a competitive squad later.

DiSE (Diploma in Sporting Excellence)
A two-year Swim England programme for talented 16–18 year olds, combining full-time education with high-level swimming support. Part of the Talent pathway.

DofE (Duke of Edinburgh's Award)
A youth award programme. Volunteering at Leander can count towards the volunteering section. Students arrange it with the club themselves, and their DofE leader confirms the level.

DS swimming
Swimming for people with Down Syndrome, which has its own European and World Championships.

G

Gala / meet
A swimming competition. Both words mean the same thing.

GB Trials
The championships where Great Britain teams are selected.

I

IM (individual medley)
A race where one swimmer swims all four strokes in order: butterfly, backstroke, breaststroke, freestyle.

J

Judge 1
The first Swim England judging qualification. Open from age 15: an online course, a mentored trainee period, then qualification, usually within a year.

K

Kickboard
A float held in front of the swimmer, so they can practise kicking with their legs only.

L

LC (long course)
Racing or training in a 50m pool.

Level 1–4 meet
Swim England licence levels for competitions. Level 4 is for newer racers; Level 1 is national level. Times at licensed meets count for rankings and qualifying.

Licensed meet
A competition approved by Swim England, so swimmers' times are official. A meet needs enough qualified officials to be licensed.

Long-term athlete development
Matching training to a swimmer's age and stage. Our S&C uses three stages: Learn to Train (9–12), Train to Train (12–16) and Train to Compete (16+).

M

Masters
Our adult squad (18+) for fitness swimmers, triathletes and people returning to the water.

Medley relay
A relay where each of four swimmers swims a different stroke.

Mercury
Our top competitive squad, for regional and national-level swimmers. Mercury swimmers also lead warm-ups and act as role models.

O

Official / technical volunteer
A trained volunteer, such as a timekeeper, judge or referee. Without officials, meets can't run, and times don't count.

Open meet
A competition that swimmers from many clubs can enter.

P

PB (personal best)
A swimmer's fastest ever time in an event.

Pathway
The route through Leander: Academy stages, then Pre-Squad, then the squads (Sparks, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Mercury), with Diamond, Masters and Beyond the pool alongside.

Platinum
Our second most senior competitive squad. The entry standard includes county qualifying times.

Pre-Squad
The final Academy stage and the entry point to competitive swimming. Swimmers are assessed for a squad place.

Pull buoy
A float held between the legs, so swimmers can focus on their arms.

R

Race the Standards
Our online tool that compares your swimmer's PB with county qualifying times.

Referee
The senior official in charge of a meet.

S

S&C (strength and conditioning)
Training on land to support swimming. Ours is delivered with our partner Move4Sport.

SC (short course)
Racing or training in a 25m pool.

Season
Our swimming year follows three phases: build and develop (to winter championships), build and qualify (to GB Trials and regionals), then race and peak (to summer nationals).

Sparks
Our youngest competitive squad, and the first step after the Academy.

Squad
A competitive training group, grouped by ability and age.

Squad standards
What a swimmer needs to be able to do to join each squad, including typical age, entry standard and sessions per week.

Swim England
The national governing body for swimming in England.

SwimMark and Clubmark
Accreditations that show a club meets recognised standards for how it's run and how it looks after members.

swimmingresults.org
Swim England's official rankings website. It shows every time a swimmer has swum at a licensed meet, their PBs, their county, regional, national and British rankings by age group and event, entries for national events, and a Swim England membership check. See swimmingresults explained.

T

Talent pathway
Swim England's national route for spotting and developing England's most promising young swimmers, through development camps run by counties, regions and Swim England. Swimmers are selected, not by application.

TeamUnify
Our members' system, used for the calendar, gala entries, accounts and payments.

Timekeeper
An official who times swimmers with a stopwatch at the end of a lane.

Tumble turn
A fast turn at the wall: a forward somersault, then push off. Taught in Academy Stage 6.

U

Underwaters
The fast kicking a swimmer does underwater after a start or turn, before surfacing.

UKSCA
The UK Strength and Conditioning Association, the professional body for S&C coaches.

W

Wavepower
Swim England's safeguarding policy, which we follow. It sets out how clubs keep children safe.

Welfare Officer
The person at the club you can talk to about any worry about a swimmer's safety or wellbeing. Email welfare@leanderswim.com.

World Aquatics points (formerly FINA points)
A score that compares swims across events, ages and genders. Swimming the event's base time, set from the world record, scores 1,000 points. Universities and US colleges use points to compare swimmers, and Swim England uses them when selecting swimmers for the Talent pathway.

Missing a word? Email admin@leanderswim.com and we'll add it.
