Safeguarding and policies

Every swimmer at Leander should feel safe, welcome and respected. Here's who to talk to, how we keep swimmers safe, and the rules we all agree to.

Talk to a Welfare Officer

Worried about a swimmer? Talk to us

Our Welfare Officers are here for swimmers, parents, coaches and volunteers. If something doesn't feel right, however small, please tell them. You won't be wasting anyone's time.

How we keep swimmers safe: Wavepower

Wavepower is Swim England's safeguarding policy. Safeguarding means protecting children from harm and making sure they can enjoy their sport safely. Leander has adopted Wavepower, so we follow the same standards as every Swim England club.

In practice, that means:

  • we have named Welfare Officers that anyone can contact
  • concerns are taken seriously and handled in line with Wavepower
  • everyone involved with the club agrees to a code of conduct
  • we have clear rules on photos, data and how we communicate with young people

confirm checks required for coaches, teachers and volunteers, e.g. DBS and safeguarding training

Swim England – Wavepower

Codes of conduct

Everyone at Leander agrees to a code of conduct. Each one sets out your rights and your responsibilities.

  • Code of Conduct for Children
  • Code of Conduct for Parents or Guardians
  • Code of Conduct for Coaches and Teachers
  • Code of Conduct for Members, Committee Members, Officials and Volunteers

Club policies

  • Photography Policy: how and when photos and videos of swimmers can be taken and used
  • Data Protection Policy: how we look after personal information
  • Privacy Notice: what personal data we hold and why
  • Records Storage and Retention Policy: how long we keep records
  • Refund and Cancellation Policy: your 14-day cooling-off period, and notice periods for leaving

Photos of swimmers

We only take and use photos or videos of swimmers with consent: from the swimmer if they're over 18, or from a parent or guardian if they're under 18. You can withdraw consent at any time. On this website, we name swimmers under 18 by first name and surname initial only. To check or change your consents, email admin@leanderswim.com. Read the Photography Policy for the full rules.

Questions?

For anything about safeguarding or a swimmer's wellbeing, email welfare@leanderswim.com. For questions about our other policies, email admin@leanderswim.com.
