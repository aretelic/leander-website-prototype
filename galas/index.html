Galas

Leander galas 2027

Three galas, every step explained

In 2027 Leander is hosting three galas of our own: two Academy galas for our youngest swimmers, and a Level 3 open meet for squad swimmers from Leander and visiting clubs. Each one is a step on the ladder, from a first race to times that count for rankings.

New to galas? Start with How galas work.

Help make our galas happen

Every gala needs volunteers, and licensed meets need qualified officials (trained volunteers such as timekeepers, judges and referees). Without them, meets can't run and times don't count.

Volunteer at a Leander gala

Other galas

Leander swimmers race at many other galas through the year, from internal club galas and the Annual Squad Relay to county, regional and national championships.
