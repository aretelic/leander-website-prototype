The Mercury Open

Race fast. Set PBs. Take the next step.

Leander's Swim England Level 3 open meet, for developing swimmers from Leander and visiting clubs. Upper-limit times keep the racing fair: if you're already faster than the limit for an event, you can't enter it, so everyone races swimmers at a similar level.

What is a Level 3 meet?

Swim England licenses open meets at four levels. Level 4 is for newer racers, and Level 1 is national level. Level 3 sits in between: it's the next step for swimmers who've raced at club galas and Level 4 meets.

Because the meet is licensed, every time counts. Times go into the national rankings and can be used as qualifying times (the times needed to enter county, regional or national championships).

Upper-limit times are the fastest a swimmer can be to enter an event. They're not entry standards to reach: they're a ceiling, so the meet stays focused on developing swimmers.

What to expect (especially for first-timers)

  • Warm-up before each session. Warm-ups are short and poolside space is tight, so arrive on time. warm-up times and lane allocations
  • Heats, no finals. confirm format Each race is a heat. Results are decided on time across all the heats in an age group, so you can win a medal from any heat.
  • Seeded slowest to fastest. confirm Swimmers race others of a similar speed.
  • Over-the-top starts. confirm The next heat starts while the previous swimmers are still in the water, which keeps the meet moving.
  • Swimmers sit with their club team and coaches. Parents watch from the spectator area.
  • No penalty for swimming fast. confirm If a swimmer beats the upper-limit time on the day, the swim still counts.

Awards

confirm awards

Proposed:

  • Medals for 1st, 2nd and 3rd in each age group and event
  • Golden Heat: one heat per session is drawn at random after it's swum, and its winner gets a prize. Any swimmer, in any heat, can win
  • PB wall: swimmers who set a personal best add their name to the wall on poolside first name and club only, in line with our naming policy
  • Top points: a prize for the best swim of the meet by World Aquatics points (a score that compares swims across events, ages and genders)

Volunteers and officials needed

A licensed meet can't run without qualified officials: trained volunteers such as timekeepers, judges and referees. If there aren't enough, times don't count.

  • Visiting clubs: please offer licensed officials for one or more sessions. officials' organiser contact
  • Leander families: we need timekeepers, marshals, runners and help with setup, refreshments and spectator tickets. volunteer sign-up link
  • Want to become an official? Start with the online Judge 1 course (from age 15). See Volunteer & become an official.
