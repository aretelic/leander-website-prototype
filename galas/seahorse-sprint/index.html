What to expect (especially if it's your first gala)

Is my child ready?
which Academy stages can enter and what swimmers need to be able to do, e.g. swim 25m unaided and start in the water. If you're not sure, ask your child's teacher.

How the day works

WhenWhat happens
ArriveFind the Leander team and drop your swimmer with the teachers. where swimmers gather and where parents sit
Warm-upSwimmers warm up in the pool with their teachers
MarshallingBefore each race, swimmers are called to a waiting area and taken to the right lane
RaceSwimmers race one length or more, depending on their stage
FinishEvery finisher gets award for every finisher, e.g. ribbon or certificate

What to bring: Leander hat, costume, goggles, a towel, warm clothes, a water bottle and a snack. See How galas work.

For parents: cheer them on, then let the teachers do the rest. Let the coach coach. Let the swimmer swim. Let the parent support.

Events and age groups

event list for each stage

Ideas to confirm:

  • 25m races in each stroke the swimmers have learned
  • A kick race and a fun relay
  • Races grouped by Academy stage or age grouping

Awards (ideas to confirm)

  • Something for every finisher ribbon, certificate or both
  • Lucky Shell: one heat is drawn at random, and its winner gets a prize. A slower swimmer can win too
  • Best streamline and Biggest smile, chosen by the teachers
  • Most improved across both Academy galas, presented at this gala confirm series award

Volunteers needed

An Academy gala needs lots of helpers. No experience needed for most roles, and we'll show you what to do.

RoleWhat you do
TimekeeperTime one lane with a stopwatch. Training given on the day confirm on-the-day timekeeper training is offered
MarshalHelp swimmers find their race and lane
RunnerTake result slips from poolside to the results desk
Welcome and setupHelp set up, greet families and pack away

Squad swimmers can get officiating experience at our Academy galas. From age 15, they can start the Judge 1 course to become a qualified official. See Beyond the pool.
