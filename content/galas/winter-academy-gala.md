## Volunteers needed

An Academy gala needs lots of helpers. No experience needed for most roles, and we'll show you what to do.

| Role | What you do |
|---|---|
| Timekeeper | Time one lane with a stopwatch. Training given on the day {{TBC: confirm on-the-day timekeeper training is offered}} |
| Marshal | Help swimmers find their race and lane |
| Runner | Take result slips from poolside to the results desk |
| Welcome and setup | Help set up, greet families and pack away |

**Squad swimmers** can get officiating experience at our Academy galas. From age 15, they can start the Judge 1 course to become a qualified official. See [Beyond the pool].
