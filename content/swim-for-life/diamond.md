# Diamond

## Keep swimming, your way, and stay part of the team

Diamond is our non-competitive squad for teenagers. Swim for fitness, swim with your friends, or ease off racing while you get through exams. Lots of our swimmers choose it, and it's a choice we value as much as racing.

## Who it's for

- **Teenagers who love swimming but don't want to race.** You get proper coached training, without galas or qualifying times. (A *qualifying time* is the time you need to swim to enter a championship.)
- **Swimmers who want to keep fit and stay with their friends.** Same club, same team, same people.
- **Competitive swimmers easing off for GCSEs, A levels or other exams.** Drop your training load for a while without leaving the club.

## What it's like

Diamond sessions are coached squad training. You'll work on fitness and technique in a group of swimmers of a similar age, but there's no expectation to race and no squad standard to meet. (*Squad standards* are what each competitive squad expects before a swimmer moves up; Diamond doesn't have one.)

**Sessions per week**

| Minimum | Recommended |
|---|---|
| 1 | 3 |

One session a week keeps you in the water and part of the team. Three a week is what we recommend if you want to stay fit and keep your skills sharp.

## Easing off for exams

Exam years are busy. Our advice to every family is the same: **keep a routine and adjust the load, rather than stopping.** Swimming is a healthy break from study.

**How it works**

1. **Tell your coach early.** The sooner we know about exams, the easier it is to plan.
2. **Agree a plan together.** For some swimmers that means fewer sessions in their competitive squad. For others it means moving to Diamond for a term or a year.
3. **Keep swimming.** Even one session a week helps you hold on to fitness and skills, and keeps you connected to your friends at the club.
4. **Come back when you're ready.** After exams, talk to your coach about moving back to a competitive squad.

## Moving between Diamond and the competitive squads

Moving between Diamond and a competitive squad is normal, and it works both ways.

**From a competitive squad to Diamond**
- Your coach will talk it through with you and your family.
- Each competitive squad has a minimum number of sessions a week. If a swimmer can't meet it for a while, for example in an exam year, Diamond is a good way to keep training at a lower load.

## Questions parents ask

**Is Diamond a step down?**
No. Diamond is a different goal, and we value it just as much as racing.

**My child is in a competitive squad but has GCSEs coming up. What should we do?**
Tell the coach as early as you can. Together you can decide whether to reduce sessions in their current squad or move to Diamond for a while. Either way, we'd encourage them to keep a routine rather than stop.

**Can my child go back to their old squad after exams?**
Yes, moving back is possible. Talk to the coach about timing. {{TBC: confirm whether a place in the previous squad is held, or whether it depends on standards and space}}

**Does Diamond swim in galas?**
Diamond is non-competitive, so there's no expectation to race. {{TBC: can Diamond swimmers enter club galas or the Annual Squad Relay if they want to?}}

**How many sessions does my child need to come to?**
At least 1 a week. We recommend 3.

**Can Diamond swimmers help out at the club?**
Yes. Many teenagers volunteer, teach or train as officials. See [Beyond the pool].
