# Masters

## Coached swimming for adults, whatever your reason for being in the water

Masters is Leander's squad for adults aged 18 and over. Get fitter, train for a triathlon, or get back in the pool after a few years away, with qualified coaches on poolside.

## Who it's for

- **Adults aged 18 and over.**
- **Fitness swimmers** who want structured, coached sessions instead of swimming lengths on their own.
- **Triathletes** who want to improve their swim, with coaches who specialise in triathlon.
- **Returners:** former club swimmers, or anyone getting back in the water after time away.

Two coached sessions a week. A coach plans each session and gives you feedback on your stroke, so every length has a purpose.
