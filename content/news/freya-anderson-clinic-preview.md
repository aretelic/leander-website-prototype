### Out of the pool: being a female athlete
Alongside the swimming, **Dr Emily Jevons** will lead an education session on:

- **Nutrition:** fuelling for training and racing
- **Training around the menstrual cycle:** understanding periods and how to train and compete well throughout the month

These are topics that matter to every young athlete, and that too often go unspoken. Knowing how your body works helps you train well and stay healthy for longer.
