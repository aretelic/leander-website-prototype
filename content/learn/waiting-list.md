# Join the Academy waiting list

Add your child's details and we'll be in touch when a place may be coming up. It takes about three minutes.

[PHOTO: kickboard on the poolside, academy lesson]

## What happens next

1. **You join the list** using the form below. We'll email to confirm we've got it.
2. **When a place may be coming up,** we'll contact you to arrange a short trial, so we can see which stage suits your child. Trials run throughout the year. {{TBC: confirm how families are contacted and how the trial works}}
3. **If there's a place,** your child starts in the right stage, and you pay the £48.75 annual membership plus the termly fee. See [Fees and term dates](/learn/fees-and-term-dates).
