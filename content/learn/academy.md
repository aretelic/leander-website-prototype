More than 300 children aged 5 to 10 learn with us each week at Ernest Bevin in Tooting. They're taught in small groups by teachers who all have a competitive swimming background, and they move up a stage as soon as they're ready.

## Who it's for

- Children aged **5–10**, from complete beginners to confident swimmers.
- Families who want their child to **swim safely and well**. That's a great reason to join, and it's why many of our families are here.
- Children who might one day enjoy **racing**. The Academy is run inside a competitive club, so there's a direct route into our squads if they want it.
- Children who enjoy **other water sports**. The skills transfer to any aquatic sport.

## What it's like

The Academy has more than 300 swimmers and is taught at **Ernest Bevin College in Tooting**. Lessons follow the school year (September to July) and carry on through half-term.

Children learn in small groups of up to 10, matched to their stage. Every one of our teachers has a competitive swimming background, so technique is taught properly from the very first lesson.

We follow the Swim England Learn to Swim Framework, adapted so that every swimmer can reach their potential. In practice, that means the basics are taught carefully, good habits are built early, and children leave each stage with skills that last.

From 2026/27, **parents are welcome back poolside** to watch lessons.

## The stages {#the-stages}

Each stage has a clear goal. Here's what your child will be able to do by the end of each one. The ages are a guide: children move up when they're ready, not on their birthday.

### Stage 1 · around age 5
**Water confidence and safety**
Your child will be able to:
- get in and out of the pool safely
- float on their own
- put their face in the water and go under (submerge)
- kick 10m on their front and on their back, using floats

### Stage 2 · around age 5
**Swimming on their own**
Your child will be able to:
- swim 15m on their own, on their front and on their back
- push off the wall and glide in a streamlined position
- practise the first drills (short skill exercises) for front crawl and backstroke

### Stage 3 · around age 6
**Good technique, front and back**
Your child will be able to:
- swim front crawl and backstroke with correct technique
- swim 25m without help, in both shallow and deep water

### Stage 4 (and 4B) · around age 6–7
**Building stamina, adding breaststroke**
Your child will be able to:
- swim 25m several times in a row
- start learning breaststroke
- dive in
- use the underwater phase: the streamlined glide and kick after a dive or push-off

### Stage 5 · around age 7–8
**All four strokes**
Your child will be able to:
- swim 50m without stopping
- start learning butterfly
- swim all four strokes: front crawl, backstroke, breaststroke and butterfly

Lessons are 30 minutes, in groups of up to 10.

### Stage 6 · around age 8–9
**Swimming like a competitive swimmer**
Your child will be able to:
- swim all four strokes with precise technique
- do legal turns (turns that follow competition rules), including tumble turns
- start from the blocks (the starting platforms used in races)
- swim individual medley (IM): all four strokes in one swim
- swim 500m or more in a lesson

Stage 6 swimmers have lessons twice a week.

### Pre-Squad · around age 9
**The first step into competitive swimming**
Your child will be able to:
- swim all strokes, turns and IM with confidence
- train "off the clock": use the pool's pace clock to time their swims and rest, the way squad swimmers do

Pre-Squad trains twice a week for 45 minutes. At the end, swimmers are assessed for a place in one of our squads. If another club would suit them better, we'll tell you honestly and help you find it.

## Questions parents ask

**My child just wants to learn to swim. Is the Academy right for us?**
Yes. Learning to swim safely and well is a great reason to join, and many of our families are here for exactly that. Being part of a competitive club means your child is taught technique properly, but there's no expectation to race.

**How long is the waiting list?**
Places are limited and waiting lists are common. A typical wait is {{TBC: typical wait for an Academy place}}. Trials run throughout the year, so join the list and we'll be in touch when a place may be coming up.

**Which stage will my child start in?**
We'll see your child swim at a short trial and place them in the stage that matches what they can do, not just their age.

**Can I watch lessons?**
Yes. From 2026/27, parents are welcome back poolside.

**Do I have to sign up for the whole year?**
Fees are paid by the term. You have 14 days after joining to change your mind; after that, Academy term fees aren't refundable. → [Fees and term dates](/learn/fees-and-term-dates)

**What's an Academy gala?**
A gala is a swimming competition. Our Academy galas give young swimmers a first chance to try racing alongside other Academy swimmers. We're hosting two in 2027. {{TBC: 2027 Academy gala dates and which stages take part}}
