# Since 1949

Leander started with 42 people at Balham Baths. Today it's more than 500 swimmers across South-West London. A lot has changed in between, but not the idea at the heart of it: a club where different swimmers, with different goals, are one team.

## How it began

Leander was founded in **1949 at Balham Baths**, as a combined water polo and swimming club. There were **42 founding members**.

It was meant to be a men-only club. The women had other ideas. They pushed to join, and Leander became a mixed club, which it has been ever since.

They wasted no time. Within six months, one of those new members broke the SCASA (Southern Counties) 220-yard freestyle record, in 2:51.6.

---

## The 1950s: national champions

The young club quickly made its mark on the national stage.

- **1951:** 15 Leander swimmers competed at the National Championships.
- **1952:** a Leander swimmer won a national title, and later set an English record in the 220-yard butterfly.
- **1955:** our women's team won the freestyle relay and came second in the medley relay at the National Championships.

## The relay that wouldn't give up

One story says a lot about Leander. Our 13/14 boys' 50m freestyle relay team started as **reserves** for the National Championships, meaning they'd only race if another team dropped out. In less than a month they went from reserves to **National Champions**.

The club only had five boys in that age group. Four of them swam. {{TBC: year of the relay win, and whether (and how) the relay swimmers can be named}}

It's the kind of thing that happens when a small group of swimmers believes in each other.

## The recent era

In more recent years, Leander has:

- been named **Top Club at the Surrey Championships twice** {{TBC: years}}
- produced numerous District and Regional champions, and won more than 100 medals {{TBC: period these figures cover}}
- produced national gold medallists {{TBC: years, and naming consent}}
- had swimmers selected for **GB juniors** and the **British Potential Programme** {{TBC: years, and naming consent}}

In 2022, six Leander swimmers made their debut at the English and British Championships, bringing home **2 golds, 6 finals, 4 club records and 12 PBs** (personal bests). One of them won English National titles in the 400m and 800m freestyle.

That same season, the club set itself a simple goal: becoming *a club again*, bringing together swimmers, parents and families, coaches and teachers, volunteers and the committee.

## Today

[PHOTO: wide shot of a busy Leander squad session from the balcony, swimmers non-identifiable]

Leander today is a not-for-profit, volunteer-run club with **more than 500 swimmers**: over 300 in our Academy learning to swim, and 210 in our squads.

In the 2025/26 season, our swimmers:

| | |
|---|---|
| **55** | qualified for championships |
| **29** | reached finals |
| **25** | won medals |
| **29** | set new club records |

Two swimmers qualified for **GB Trials** (the championships where Great Britain teams are selected) and both reached finals. Two qualified for **GB Summer Championships**, winning a medal.

And the club is growing. Our squads grew from **118 to 143 swimmers** between September 2025 and October 2026, not counting Sparks (our youngest squad) or Diamond (our non-competitive squad).

## What hasn't changed

The women who pushed to join in 1949 would recognise the club today. It's still a place where people turn up, train hard and look after each other. Our history isn't a trophy cabinet. It's a reminder that this club has always been made by the people in it.
