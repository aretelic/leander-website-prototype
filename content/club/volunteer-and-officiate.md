# Volunteer and become an official

Leander is run by volunteers. Whether you can give an afternoon a year or a morning a month, your help keeps the club going and makes your swimmer's races count.

[PHOTO: timekeeper with a stopwatch at the end of a lane, hands and clipboard only, non-identifiable]

---

## Why we need you

**Officials** are trained volunteers, such as timekeepers, judges and referees. Without enough of them, a meet (a swimming competition) can't be licensed by Swim England, and **the times swum there don't count** for rankings or qualifying.

So when you volunteer as an official, you're not just helping out. You're making sure every swimmer's hard work is recognised. It's also a rewarding way to be at the meets your swimmer races in.

## The officials pathway, step by step

You don't need to know anything about swimming rules to start. You'll learn as you go, alongside experienced officials.

| Step | What happens | How long |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Sign up** | Tell us you're interested. We'll register you and explain the next steps. | {{TBC: how to register, e.g. Swim England membership needed}} |
| **2. Online Judge 1 course** | Complete Swim England's online Judge 1 course. Open to anyone aged 15 or over, so teenage swimmers can do it too. | {{TBC: course length}} |
| **3. Mentored trainee** | Work at galas as a trainee, with an experienced official mentoring you. | Over several galas |
| **4. Qualified Judge 1** | Once signed off, you're a qualified Judge 1. | Usually within a year of starting |
| **5. Go further** | Experienced officials can progress to higher qualifications, such as referee. | {{TBC: next qualifications and support available}} |

**What's a Judge 1?** It's the first Swim England judging qualification. A Judge 1 watches swimmers to check that strokes, turns and finishes follow the rules.

**What's a timekeeper?** A timekeeper stands at the end of a lane and times each swimmer with a stopwatch. It's often where new volunteers start. {{TBC: confirm whether timekeeping needs its own course at the club}}
