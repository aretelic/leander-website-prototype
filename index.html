Leander Swimming Club · South London · since 1949

Swim your best, among friends

We help children become confident, fit, capable swimmers, with friendships and achievements that carry them through school and beyond. From first lessons at five to national finals, in a club that's friendly, supportive and proud of all of them.

Ages 5 to 10

Learn to swim

Our Academy teaches children to swim well, the way competitive swimmers are taught, in small groups at Ernest Bevin. Plenty of families join simply to raise a strong, safe swimmer.

Teens and adults

Swim for Life

Diamond lets teenagers keep training without the pressure of racing, including through exams. Masters is for adults. Swimmers with a disability, including Down Syndrome, train with us in our squads.

About Swim for Life

What swimming at Leander gives you or your child

Swimming is a life skill first. Done well, it also shapes who you become: fit, confident, and used to working hard for something you care about. That's as true for our Masters swimmers as for our five-year-olds.

Fit and strong for every sport

Swimming works the heart, lungs and every major muscle group, and it's gentle on growing joints. That fitness carries straight over to rugby, hockey, netball, running and the rest.

Confidence they carry into school

Setting a goal, training for it and racing under pressure builds a quiet self-belief. Children learn that steady effort pays off, and that habit serves them well in the classroom and in exams.

Friends across schools and ages

Our swimmers come from schools all over South London, and the younger and older squads cheer each other on at galas. Many of those friendships last well beyond the pool.

Works alongside school sport

Leander training fits around school swimming, water polo and swim teams, and a school squad session counts towards a swimmer's weekly training. Plenty of our swimmers do both.

How the pathway works

Most swimmers start in the Academy and move up when they're ready. Swimmers can switch to Diamond or Masters at any point, and many of our older swimmers go on to teach in the Academy.

A good year in the pool

In 2025/26 our swimmers qualified for championships 55 times, reached 29 finals and won 25 medals. Two raced at GB Trials, and between them our swimmers broke 29 club records.

We're just as proud of the eight-year-old who finally nails a tumble turn.

Beyond the pool

Swimming at Leander leads to more than times. Our squad swimmers can train as paid Academy teachers, learn to officiate at our own galas, volunteer towards their Duke of Edinburgh's Award, lead warm-ups for the younger squads, and take their swimming on to university.

A proper club, run by the people in it

Alongside training there's the Annual Squad Relay, where nine-year-olds race alongside seventeen-year-olds, Academy galas for first-time racers, a summer social and our Tenerife training camp, helped along by cake sales on Saturday mornings at Ernest Bevin.

Leander is run by volunteers, so if you can spare a little time, we'd love your help.
