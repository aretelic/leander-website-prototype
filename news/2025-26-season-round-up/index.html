2025/26: our season in numbers

News · publication date

What a season. From county championships to GB Trials, Leander swimmers raced at every level of the sport, and the club grew along the way.

Book a squad trialJoin the Academy waiting list

The headline numbers

55
swimmers qualified for championships
29
reached finals
25
won medals
29
new club records, in long course and short course

What do these mean? To qualify for a championship, a swimmer has to swim a set qualifying time at an earlier licensed meet. A final is the race for the fastest swimmers after the heats. And long course and short course simply mean 50m and 25m pools, which have separate records.

Where our swimmers raced

The season took our swimmers up every level of the competition ladder:

  • Counties: the Surrey County Championships, the first step into championship racing
  • Winter and Summer Regionals: the London regional championships
  • Winter Nationals: national-level racing
  • GB Trials: where Great Britain teams are selected. Two Leander swimmers qualified, and both reached finals.
  • GB Summer Championships: two swimmers qualified, and one came home with a medal.

swimmer highlights with consent

A club that's growing

Results are only part of the story. Our squads grew from 118 to 143 swimmers between September 2025 and October 2026 (not counting Sparks, our youngest squad, or Diamond, our non-competitive squad). Today we have 210 squad swimmers and more than 300 in our Academy.

We also said goodbye to nine Mercury swimmers who left for university. We're proud of every one of them, and they'll always be part of the team.

Thank you

Behind every one of these swims is a team: coaches who plan every session, parents who drive to early mornings, and volunteers and officials who give up their weekends so meets can run and times can count.

quote from Head Coach Edgar Andrade

What's next

The 2026/27 season is already under way, with our Olympian clinic with Freya Anderson in January and our Tenerife training camp ahead.
