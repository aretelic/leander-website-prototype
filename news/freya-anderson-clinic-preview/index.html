Olympian Freya Anderson is coming to Leander

News · publication date

On Saturday 9 January 2027, Team GB Olympian Freya Anderson will lead a clinic for our Platinum and Mercury swimmers at Ernest Bevin College, our home pool in Tooting.

Athlete supportBook a squad trial

Who is Freya Anderson?

Freya swam for Team GB at the Tokyo and Paris Olympic Games. approved bio and achievements for Freya Anderson

What's happening on the day

When
Saturday 9 January 2027, start and finish times
Where
Ernest Bevin College, Beechcroft Road, Tooting SW17 7DF
Who
30 swimmers from Platinum and Mercury, our two most senior competitive squads
What
A clinic with Freya clinic format, e.g. pool session and Q&A, plus an education session with Dr Emily Jevons

With Freya

what the session with Freya will cover

Out of the pool: being a female athlete

Alongside the swimming, Dr Emily Jevons will lead an education session on:

  • Nutrition: fuelling for training and racing
  • Training around the menstrual cycle: understanding periods and how to train and compete well throughout the month

These are topics that matter to every young athlete, and that too often go unspoken. Knowing how your body works helps you train well and stay healthy for longer.

one-line description of Dr Emily Jevons

Part of something bigger

This clinic is part of our athlete education programme. Our top squads take part in regular clinics with Olympians, and learn about the things that help swimmers thrive beyond the pool, from balancing school and training to being a female athlete.

Beyond the pool: what swimming at Leander leads to

For parents of swimmers taking part

  • Places: 30 places, for Platinum and Mercury swimmers. how places are allocated
  • What to bring: kit and what to bring
  • Cost: cost, if any
  • Questions: speak to your swimmer's coach or email admin@leanderswim.com

We'll share a full report after the clinic.
